# Between Us — Metadata Inventory & Classification Specification

> **Phase 17 Privacy & Metadata Specification**  
> **Version:** 1.0.0  
> **Status:** Authoritative Metadata Inventory

---

## 1. Overview & Architectural Distinction

**Between Us** is engineered as a private 1-to-1 WebRTC audio/video communication system. Privacy architecture requires a strict, honest distinction between two metadata realms:

1. **Application-Controlled Metadata**: Data generated or processed directly by the Between Us Next.js client, WebRTC state machine, and signaling client. This data is strictly minimized, kept ephemeral in volatile RAM, and deleted immediately upon session termination.
2. **Infrastructure / Network Metadata**: Lower-level transport and operational data generated by external network layers (IP packet headers, TLS handshakes, Cloudflare DNS, Coturn TURN relay VPS, Vercel edge routers). This data is inherent to TCP/UDP networking and is documented accurately without false claims of "zero metadata".

## 3. Prohibited Metadata Categories

The Following Metadata Categories Are **STRICTLY PROHIBITED** From Creation, Collection, or Persistence:

- ❌ **Call History Records**: Zero database records of past calls, missed calls, or call durations.
- ❌ **Analytics & Telemetry**: Zero Google Analytics, Plausible, Mixpanel, Sentry, or tracking pixels.
- ❌ **Browser Fingerprinting**: Zero Canvas, WebGL, AudioContext, or font enumeration fingerprinting.
- ❌ **Tracking Cookies**: Zero third-party tracking or advertising cookies.
- ❌ **Persistent Session Correlation**: Zero master per-user tracking GUID linking successive call sessions.
- ❌ **Call Content Storage**: Zero audio recordings, video recordings, screenshots, or thumbnails.
